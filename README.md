
##  Convolutional neural networks can identify brain interactions involved in decoding spatial auditory attention 🧠🎧


In a crowded room where multiple conversations are happening simultaneously, human listeners have the ability to focus and attend on a single speaker and ignore the others. Our brain does so by employing certain frequencies, temporal patterns and through complex interactions between brain regions. 

In this project we present a novel brain-inspired Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) architecture designed to decode the direction of attention using just 5 seconds of EEG data. 

### 🌐 Project Highlights:

- **Innovative CNN Architecture:** We've crafted a transparent CNN inspired by the complex properties of brain signals and the dynamic interplay between brain regions.

- **Brain-Inspired Design:** Instead of a black-box network, our architecture mimics the brain's functionality, leveraging its signal processing capabilities to enhance CNN performance.

- **New Brain Modelling Approach:** Our approach demonstrates how state-of-the-art CNN architectures can unlock new insights into brain functioning and cognitive processes.
